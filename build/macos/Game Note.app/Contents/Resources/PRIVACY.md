# Game Note Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Game Note is a local match-video review application published by Hangzhou Amuswen Network Technology Co., Ltd. ("Em7 Games", "we", or "us").

## Data handling

Game Note does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. The app does not send your videos, annotations, project files, or usage data to us.

The app accesses videos and project folders only when you select them. Videos, drawings, notes, topic organization, and project metadata are processed and stored on your device in locations you choose.

## External services

The About screen contains links for email support, source code, and legal documents. Opening one of these links leaves the app and may provide information to your email provider, browser, GitHub, or another service according to that service's privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

Because project data remains on your device, you control its retention. You can delete a Game Note project by deleting its project folder. Removing the app does not automatically delete projects stored elsewhere on your device.

## Children

Game Note does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact [account@em7games.com](mailto:account@em7games.com).
